# FlandersHax

### A Hack Club community in Flanders, Belgium.


Welcome to **FlandersHax** — a community for young makers, developers, programmers, and tech enthusiasts in Flanders!


 **Our websites:**
- [flandershax.com](https://flandershax.com)
- [flandershax.xo.je](https://flandershax.xo.je)

---

##  What is FlandersHax?

FlandersHax is a Hack Club community focused on bringing people together through technology and creativity.

Whether you're an experienced developer or writing your first line of code, there's a place for you here.

Our interests include:

-  Programming and software development
-  Hardware and electronics
-  Robotics and embedded systems
-  Web development
-  Game development
-  Open-source projects and experimentation

##  Our Mission

We want to make technology more accessible, encourage creativity, and help young people turn their ideas into real projects.

We believe the best way to learn is to build something yourself.

No experience required. Just curiosity and a willingness to create.
